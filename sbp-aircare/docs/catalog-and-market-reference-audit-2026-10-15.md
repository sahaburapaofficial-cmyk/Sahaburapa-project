Reviewed through 16 October 2026. This note records what was verified and where competitor pages were used as presentation references. It is not a price approval or a claim that these are the five highest Google organic results.

## Product data added to the site

The development catalog contains 705 model rows. The C14/C15 evidence handoff covers a 1,159-model master; those records are not automatically company-approved products or Pricebook offers. The current evidence index holds 356 exact catalog/model matches, 7,921 source-backed field values (7,801 manufacturer and 120 Thai industrial-standard license values), and 166 source records. It covers AUX, Carrier, Casper, Central Air, Daikin, Eminent, Haier, Hitachi, Midea, Mitsubishi Electric, Mitsubishi Heavy Duty, Panasonic, Saijo Denki, Sharp, and TCL. Regulatory records are labeled separately from OEM sources. These are source-backed additions in the development branch, not a claim that a live release has been published.

Field coverage is intentionally uneven: 316 models have an exact manufacturer BTU rating; 251 have all three indoor-unit dimensions (width, height, and depth); 243 have a source-backed power-supply record; and 143 state rated current. Other fields appear only where the model-specific source supports them. This does not mean all 356 models have a complete datasheet. The 349 current catalog rows without exact manufacturer or regulatory evidence remain listed with their existing company data; they are not described as source-verified. Exact conductor count, interconnect-cable details, or breaker rating remains unpublished unless the exact model manual supports it.

In 143 cases, the reviewed manufacturer BTU value differs from the existing company Pricebook band. The site shows the manufacturer rating as product capacity and uses the existing Pricebook BTU band separately for price and installation calculations. That band, the product price, all service rates, and the quote calculation were left unchanged. Room-fit sizing uses the manufacturer rating and exact three-axis dimensions when those fields exist; otherwise it labels the company-list or estimated dimensions accordingly.

On 16 October, 11 Saijo Denki wall models were matched against the official Turbo A.P.S R32 and Smart Cool R32 catalogue tables. The evidence includes exact indoor/outdoor codes, cooling rating, supply phase, rated input, SEER, airflow, sound levels where clearly stated, pipe sizes, maximum piping limits, equipment dimensions and weights, and main-cable cross-section. The PDF edition is **2021 (M9)**; the current official product list confirms the same families and capacity options but does not republish the technical table. The edition is visible beside the manufacturer link, and fields not clearly present in the source remain blank. See [the source capture and model-by-model comparison](SAIJO_DENKI_RESIDENTIAL_CATALOG_CAPTURE_2026-10-16.md).
